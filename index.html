Cetus · whale-aware ship routing (simulation)

Hear the whale. Locate it. Route around it.

Ships kill dozens of blue, humpback and fin whales off California every year, mostly because nobody knows exactly where the whales are. Today's answer is to slow whole regions to 10 knots. Cetus simulates what changes if each whale can be located, so a ship can make a small, targeted change instead.

  1. Watch a real shipping route, Oakland → Long Beach, through real strike hotspots.
  2. See each whale call heard, located and tracked, and every decision explained with its risk, cost, fuel and CO₂.
  3. Choose what the ship does when its own hydrophone hears a whale nobody has located (Ships/Mix): slow down, ask you, or hold speed.
  4. Compare the modes in the Fleet lab: the same whales in every mode, many voyages at once, and the business case. Click the progress bar to replay any moment.
